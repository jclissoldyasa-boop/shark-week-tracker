2a. Sharing with a partner

Partner heads-up is off unless you turn it on. When on, anyone who has your link can see your forecast, so only share it with people you trust. Turning it off deletes the forecast straight away and the old link stops working. Heads-up messages are sent by you, through your own messaging apps; we don't contact your partner.

3. How we use it

Only to provide the app to you: syncing your data, signing you in, keeping the service secure, and contacting you about your account if needed. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or to train AI models. Predictions and insights are calculated on your device.

4. Encryption and security (APP 11)

  • Your password is turned into keys on your device (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds). One key unlocks a random 256-bit data key; a separate key is used to sign in. The server never receives your password or your data key.
  • Every entry is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device. Entry names are scrambled too, so the server can't even see which dates you logged.
  • All connections use HTTPS (TLS). The service uses strict security headers, rate limiting on sign-in, and per-account access controls so one account can never read another's records.
  • On Android, the app blocks screenshots and hides its content in the recent-apps screen by default, and excludes your data from device backups.
  • Because we can't decrypt your data, we can't recover it if you lose both your password and your recovery code.

If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

5. Where your information is stored (APP 8)

We use Cloudflare, Inc. to host the app and its database. The database is set to be stored in the Oceania region, but Cloudflare is a US company with a global network, so your information (including your email address and encrypted data) may be processed in other countries while it is in transit or cached. Cloudflare acts only on our instructions and is bound by contractual data-protection terms. Your health data is encrypted before it leaves your device, so it is unreadable to Cloudflare and anyone else. By consenting to this policy you acknowledge this disclosure.
